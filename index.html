Dream Life — Privacy Policy

A personal journaling, affirmation, and self-development companion.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains exactly what Dream Life ("the app") collects, why, and what control you have.

What we collect

Account information. An email address and password are required to create an account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us. You may optionally provide a display name.

Content you create. Journal entries, gratitude notes, mood check-ins and mood notes, daily intentions, goals, affirmation practice history, meditation activity, lesson progress, badges, and settings. This content exists so the app can work for you — it is stored in our database (hosted by Supabase) and scoped to your account with row-level security, meaning only your authenticated account can read it.

Luna chat messages. When you talk to Luna, the app's AI companion, your messages (and brief context such as your recent mood and progress, used to make replies relevant) are sent through our server to Anthropic, the AI provider that powers Luna, to generate a response. Anthropic processes this data as a service provider. Chat history is stored with your account so your conversation continues across sessions.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that profile you.
  • We do not read your journals; access is technically restricted to your authenticated account.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server functions (data hosting).
  • Anthropic — AI processing for Luna chat messages, as described above.

These providers process data only to provide the app's functionality.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Your choices

  • Mood notes, intentions, journals, and chat are always optional.
  • Notifications are off unless you enable them, and can be disabled in Settings.

Children

Dream Life is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
